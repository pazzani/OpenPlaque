# LCX vs OM explicit AV-groove locus identity

This experiment is a fresh branch from frozen baseline `0593b453959f5a353d644267fbeef24b514ef4d7`.

Prerequisite: the prior source-space parent/daughter topology must have established C6 as the continuing parent trajectory, C9 as a truncation of C6, and C7 as a genuine daughter branch.

The experiment constructs an explicit AV-groove locus from cardiac anatomy rather than scoring either candidate directly. Myocardial surface points near the coronary region are selected when their distances to the atrial and ventricular chamber surfaces are both small and approximately balanced. A graph centerline is then extracted from that locus.

Controls use the same method on the right side: accepted RCA against the RA/RV groove is the positive control, while accepted LAD against the left AV-groove locus is the negative control.

C6 and C7 are scored by distance to the explicit left AV-groove centerline, tangent alignment with that centerline, downstream retention versus departure, and source-space dual-coronary-mask/HU support. Old curved-template similarity and prior local AV-groove scores have zero decision weight.

A positive result nominates one daughter as LCX-like (groove-following) and the other as OM-like (groove-departing), but does not establish clinical vessel identity and does not resolve LM.
