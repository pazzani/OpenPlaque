# LCX parent-continuation topology experiment

This experiment is a fresh branch from frozen baseline `0593b453959f5a353d644267fbeef24b514ef4d7`.

Purpose:

1. Test whether source candidate C9 is a quantitative truncated prefix of C6.
2. If so, collapse C6/C9 into one parent-continuation hypothesis represented by the longer C6 path.
3. Compare that parent against C7 using local branch topology after the Family-2 internal split.

The topology gate is independent of curved-template similarity and independent of the previous AV-groove score. C6 must continue the incoming Family-2 trunk within 25 degrees, C7 must branch by at least 35 degrees, the daughter-to-daughter angle must be at least 25 degrees, and both real paths must retain dual TotalSegmentator support and robust source-CCTA attenuation.

A positive result establishes **source-space parent-versus-daughter topology only**. It does not by itself establish clinical LCX or obtuse-marginal identity. LM remains unresolved.
