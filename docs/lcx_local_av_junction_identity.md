# LCX local AV-junction ribbon identity experiment

This experiment is a fresh branch from frozen OpenPlaque baseline `0593b453959f5a353d644267fbeef24b514ef4d7`.

Goal: adjudicate C6 versus C7 as LCX-like versus OM-like using a **local, daughter-blind atrioventricular junction ribbon** rather than a global chamber-equidistance seam.

Method:
- Reconstruct the established C6/C7/C9 Family-2 split and verify the C6/C9 truncation prerequisite.
- Around the split only, collect myocardial-surface points that are locally balanced between LA and LV and close to both chambers.
- Score C6 and C7 by distance to this ribbon, local PCA tangent alignment, downstream retention/departure, chamber balance, dual coronary-mask support, and source-CCTA HU support.
- Build an analogous right-sided ribbon around an accepted RCA segment as a positive control.
- Use the accepted LAD near the left split as a negative control.
- Curved LCX-template similarity and previous AV-groove scores have zero decision weight.

A positive result only nominates an LCX-like junction-following daughter and an OM-like junction-departing daughter. It does not establish clinical vessel identity and does not resolve LM.
