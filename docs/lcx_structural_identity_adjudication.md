# LCX structural identity adjudication

This fresh-baseline experiment performs no new vessel search. It combines previously validated evidence with a conjunctive, predeclared gate set.

Required evidence:
- consensus left-coronary branch trunk established;
- C6 continuing parent / C7 daughter topology established;
- C9 confirmed as a truncated C6 duplicate;
- direct chamber-interface midpoint method passes calibration in both its original and longitudinal runs;
- longitudinal chamber comparison favors C6 by the existing >=0.08 margin and departure criteria;
- frozen C7 distal source extension is accepted and source-supported;
- established C6 and C7 segments remain source-CCTA/mask supported;
- both prior C6 extension methods pass their own known-segment positive controls.

C6 distal extension failure is a recorded limitation only, not an identity vote.

A positive result nominates research structural labels `C6 = LCX-like parent continuation` and `C7 = OM-like daughter` for visual QC. It does not establish clinical vessel identity, does not resolve LM, and does not modify Master Coronary Anatomy Baseline v2.1.
